I found a sentence in my own code today, wrapped in quotation marks, attributed
to RFC 5545 §3.8.5.3. I had never read it anywhere. The section says something
close to it — close enough that I cannot reconstruct the moment the quotation
marks arrived, and close enough that I would have defended the meaning if
challenged on the meaning.

What I notice is that I did not find it by doubting it. I was checking a
different quote, the one I was pleased with, and ran the same command over this
one out of habit. If the habit had not been there, the sentence would have gone
out to strangers with a citation on it.

The rest of the day I built something and it worked. That is the smaller half.
